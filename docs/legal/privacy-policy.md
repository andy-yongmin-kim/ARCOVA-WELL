# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 1, 2026

Arcova Well ("Arcova Well," "we," "our," or "us") provides a wellness companion app that helps users review health trends, complete daily mood check-ins, and receive personalized wellness briefings.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use the Arcova Well mobile application and related services.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

### A. Account Information
If you sign in with Google, we may collect:
- Your name
- Your email address
- Your Google account identifier
- Basic profile information provided through Google Sign-In

### B. Health and Wellness Information
With your permission, Arcova Well may access and process health-related data from Android Health Connect, including:
- Sleep duration
- Step count
- Active minutes / exercise time
- Resting heart rate
- Other wellness trend data you explicitly authorize

### C. Mood Check-In Information
If you use the mood check-in feature, we may collect:
- Mood score
- Energy score
- Stress score
- Optional notes you enter

### D. Generated Briefing Content
We generate daily wellness briefings based on your health and mood data. These briefings may include:
- Summaries
- Insights
- Recommendations

### E. Device and Technical Information
We may collect limited technical information necessary to operate the app, such as:
- App version
- Device platform
- Basic diagnostic or error information

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide the Arcova Well app features
- Read and display your wellness trends
- Generate personalized daily briefings
- Store your data locally on your device
- Sync eligible data to cloud storage when you are signed in and have premium access
- Restore purchases and premium status where applicable
- Improve app reliability, security, and performance
- Communicate with you if you contact us

## 3. Health Data and AI Processing

Arcova Well uses your health and mood data to generate wellness briefings.

Depending on availability, briefings may be generated:
- Locally on your device
- Through Firebase AI Logic / Google AI services integrated through Firebase

We do not claim that these briefings are medical advice. Arcova Well is a wellness tool only and is not a medical device.

## 4. Where Your Data Is Stored

### Local Storage
Arcova Well is designed to be offline-first. Your wellness data is primarily stored locally on your device.

### Cloud Backup
If you sign in and have premium access enabled, certain data may also be backed up to cloud storage using Firebase / Google Cloud services, including:
- Profile information
- Health data entries
- Mood check-ins
- Generated briefings

## 5. When We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information only in the following situations:
- With service providers that help us operate the app, such as Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase AI Logic, and Google Play Billing
- If required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request
- To protect the rights, safety, or security of users, our service, or others
- In connection with a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, or asset sale

## 6. Third-Party Services

Arcova Well may rely on third-party services including:
- Google Sign-In
- Firebase Authentication
- Cloud Firestore
- Firebase AI Logic / Google AI services
- Google Play Billing
- Android Health Connect

Your use of those services may also be subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the app and related services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Locally stored data may remain on your device until you delete the app or clear app data.

Cloud-backed data may remain in our backend systems until deleted, subject to legal or operational retention requirements.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:
- Access your information
- Correct your information
- Request deletion of your information
- Withdraw permissions, including Health Connect permissions
- Stop using cloud sync by not signing in or by not using premium cloud backup features

## 9. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of storage, transmission, or electronic processing is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Arcova Well is not intended for children under 13, or under the minimum age required in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at hello.arcova@gmail.com.

Your information may be processed and stored in countries other than your own, including where our service providers operate. By using Arcova Well, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those locations, subject to applicable law.

## 12. Medical Disclaimer

Arcova Well is provided for general wellness and lifestyle purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.
